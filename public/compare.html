How to read the table

Transport encryption and end-to-end encryption

Every WebRTC connection is encrypted with DTLS. The two browsers agree on that encryption through a signaling server, so its strength depends on that server behaving honestly. DarkP2P adds a second layer whose key the server never receives, which is why its cell says more than "WebRTC". Whether that difference matters depends on how much you trust the operator of the service you use.

Storage and availability are a trade

A tool that stores your file on a server lets the recipient download it later, when you are offline. A tool that stores nothing, DarkP2P included, needs both people online at the same time with their tabs open.

Where DarkP2P is weaker

  • Both peers must be online together. There is no "send now, download later".
  • One file at a time in each direction, between exactly two peers.
  • The relay used by Tor Browser is capped at 50 MB per room without a Pro token.
  • A direct connection shows each peer the other's IP address unless one of the options to hide it is used.
