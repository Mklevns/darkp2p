Abuse statement

What the operator of darkp2p.com can and cannot do about content sent with it, and how to get in touch.

DarkP2P does not store or host files

DarkP2P connects two web browsers so that one can send a file to the other while both are online. The file is never uploaded to a DarkP2P server for storage. There is no file library, no download page and no account.

A DarkP2P link is not a link to a file. It contains a random room ID and an encryption secret, and it only does anything while two people have it open at the same time. When they close their tabs, nothing remains on the server.

The operator cannot see what is transferred

Files are encrypted in the sender's browser and decrypted in the receiver's. The secret is in the part of the link after #, which browsers do not send to the server. When a direct connection is not possible, the server forwards the encrypted data between the two browsers through memory, without the means to decrypt it and without keeping it.

The server keeps no record of who transferred what to whom. It does not log IP addresses or room IDs.

What this means for reports and requests

  • There is nothing to take down. The operator holds no copy of any transferred file and cannot remove, inspect or block a specific file.
  • There are no records to hand over. The operator cannot identify the people behind a link or a past transfer, because that information is not collected.
  • The people responsible for a transfer are the two who took part in it. If you received something unlawful through DarkP2P, the sender is whoever gave you the link, and the appropriate authority in your country is the right place to report it.

Contact

For abuse of the service itself, such as attacks on the infrastructure or misuse of the relay, for legal correspondence, and for security reports, write to:

admin@darkp2p.com

Please include what happened, when (with the time zone), and any technical detail you have. Do not send unlawful material.
